$\langle z\rangle$ = 0.29, 0.45, 0.61, 0.93

Half the shear weight has the photometry, over 3815 deg².

For validation and null tests, not for parameters.

  • Colours — the four bins, each drawn as its share of the shear weight, so they add up to the dashed total.
  • Status — a working $n(z)$ built for validation; the collaboration's $n(z)$ for parameters comes from the photo-z group.
What is plotted

DR6 BPZ photo-z bins calibrated with a SOM on the full-area v2 bound-9 sample, using either $z$ filter where available; 51% of the bound-9 shear weight is retained. Lightly smoothed for display ($\Delta z = 0.01$ grid).
