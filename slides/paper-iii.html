Paper III jointly fits cosmology and PSF leakage in real space

  • Paper III — takes the validated catalog and scale cuts from Paper II and fits a cosmological model to $\xi_\pm$.
  • Distinctive — PSF-leakage parameters are inferred from $\rho$ and $\tau$ statistics and carried into the likelihood as Gaussian priors, so PSF uncertainty propagates into the cosmological error budget (red curve).
