The 2D release: four papers from one blinded catalogue

  • Shape catalogue · Hervas-Peters et al. 2026
  • B-mode validation and scale cuts · Daley et al. 2026
  • Configuration space, $\xi_\pm(\theta)$ · Goh et al. 2026
  • Harmonic space, $C_\ell$ · Guerrini et al. 2026

About 2400 deg² effective area; $r$-band shapes, one redshift bin calibrated on external fields; blinded through $n(z)$. A core team of about ten people.

  • The four papers — one catalogue, one blinding scheme, two independent inference pipelines.
  • One redshift bin — only $r$-band was available across the footprint, so $n(z)$ comes from a SOM calibration on external deep fields.
  • Blinding — three shifted $n(z)$ realisations; scale cuts and catalogue version were frozen before unblinding.
Sources

Hervas-Peters et al. 2026, arXiv:2605.13549; Goh et al. 2026, arXiv:2605.13547; Guerrini et al. 2026, arXiv:2605.13543; Daley et al. 2026 (B modes).
