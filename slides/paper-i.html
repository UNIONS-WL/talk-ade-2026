Paper I: a shape catalog of 61 million galaxies in the northern sky

3648 deg²

$n_{\rm eff} = 4.96$ arcmin$^{-2}$

$\sigma_\epsilon = 0.27$

$r$-band, PSF FWHM 0.71″; ShapePipe metacalibration shapes.

The catalog goes public on acceptance, in the next few months.

  • Paper I — the UNIONS-3500 shape catalog (Hervas-Peters et al.): 61.4 million galaxies over 3648 deg² of CFHT $r$-band imaging.
  • Map — effective source density per HEALPix pixel ($N_{\rm side}=512$); the density drops slightly towards the Galactic plane (dust, diffuse light), and the shape noise is very homogeneous across the footprint.
  • Release — the catalog is public once the papers are accepted, in the next few months.
  • Context — $n_{\rm eff}$ is close to DES Y3 (5.6) over a comparable area, at a sharper PSF (0.71″ against 0.98″ in $r$).
