$S_8$ is robust to analysis choices but sensitive to the IA prior

Most robustness tests shift $S_8$ by $\lt 0.4\sigma$

$A_\mathrm{IA}$ is the dominant nuisance: largely unconstrained, removing it shifts $S_8$ by $0.7\sigma$

  • Robustness — scale cuts, non-linear model, covariance and leakage correction all move $S_8$ little.
  • The exception — the intrinsic-alignment treatment: $A_{\rm IA}$ follows the degeneracy with one bin; no IA $-0.71\sigma$, flat prior $+0.38\sigma$. Tomography resolves it.
