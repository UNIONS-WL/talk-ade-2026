B-modes are mitigated by cutting CCD- and large-scales

  • Suggestive of repeating CCD-scale additive bias
  • $\xi_\pm^B$, $C_\ell^{BB}$ and $B_n$ do not always agree, due to different weighting of scales
  • Paper II — B-mode validation (Daley et al.): the gatekeeper that chose the catalog version and the scale cuts.
  • Bottom row — the paper figure's $B_n/\sigma_n$ row ($E_n$ row cropped off). Shaded: modes $n > 6$; the cosmological information sits at $n \le 6$. Left — over all scales ($1$–$250'$) COSEBIs fail: the oscillating $B_n$ pattern that Asgari et al. 2019 associated with repeating additive shear bias at the CCD scale. Config- and harmonic-space COSEBIs agree, so it is the filter weighting, not the basis.
  • Right — with the fiducial cuts, $[12, 83]'$ and $\ell = 300$–$1600$, the B-modes are consistent with zero; all PTEs $> 0.18$.
  • Three statistics — over the full range $\xi_\pm^B$ and $C_\ell^{BB}$ pass while COSEBIs fail; each weights scales differently, so we require all three to pass. We could not decisively identify the origin.
