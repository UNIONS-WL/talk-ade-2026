Cosmic shear benefits from independent measurements

  • Left — published Stage III cosmic-shear $S_8$, oldest at the top; the band is the CMB.
  • Right — DES, KiDS and HSC overlap one another, almost all at or below the equator (HSC's small HECTOMAP field at Dec +43 deg is the exception). Dashed outlines show LSST WFD and Euclid Wide; the solid outline shows observed Euclid DR1.
  • Reading — the newer analyses (KiDS-Legacy, DES Y6) have moved towards the CMB as systematics control improved; nothing north of $+30^\circ$ adds an independent measurement.
Numbers

KiDS-1000 $0.759^{+0.024}_{-0.021}$; DES Y3 $0.759^{+0.025}_{-0.023}$; HSC Y3 $0.769^{+0.031}_{-0.034}$; KiDS-Legacy $0.815^{+0.016}_{-0.021}$; DES Y6 $0.798^{+0.014}_{-0.015}$; CMB $0.836^{+0.012}_{-0.013}$. Values as in the Moriond 2026 UNIONS talk.
