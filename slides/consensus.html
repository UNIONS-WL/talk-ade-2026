Cosmic shear benefits from independent measurements

  • Left — published Stage III cosmic-shear $S_8$, oldest at the top; the band is the CMB.
  • Right — where those surveys sit on the sky: DES, KiDS and HSC (filled) overlap one another, all at or below the equator; dashed are the Stage IV footprints, LSST WFD and Euclid Wide final, and Euclid DR1 as observed.
  • Reading — the newer analyses (KiDS-Legacy, DES Y6) have moved towards the CMB as systematics control improved; nothing north of $+30^\circ$ adds an independent measurement.
