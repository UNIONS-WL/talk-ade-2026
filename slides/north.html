UNIONS adds deep wide-field lensing constraints in the North

  • Purple — the UNIONS DR6 tiles, mostly above declination $+30^\circ$, with essentially no overlap with DES or KiDS; only HSC's small HECTOMAP field lies inside it.
  • Why it matters — a different telescope (CFHT/MegaCam), different on-sky systematics and independent sample variance.
  • Cross-correlations — the northern sky holds DESI spectroscopy and CMB lensing from Planck and ACT.
Footprint definitions

The full DR6 tile universe includes small equatorial extensions. It overlaps the broad DES survey envelope shown here by about 28 square degrees, and the KiDS mask by zero at the plotted resolution. The DES outline is not a selected Y3 or Y6 analysis mask.
