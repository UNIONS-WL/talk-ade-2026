UNIONS adds deep wide-field lensing constraints in the North

  • Purple — the UNIONS DR6 tiles, mostly above declination $+30^\circ$, with no overlap with DES, KiDS or HSC.
  • Why it matters — a different telescope (CFHT/MegaCam), different on-sky systematics and independent sample variance.
  • Cross-correlations — the northern sky holds DESI spectroscopy and CMB lensing from Planck and ACT.
