UNIONS-3500 constraints are the culmination of ~10 years of work

  • A small team of ~10 people
  • Non-tomographic, about 2400 deg² effective area
  • Inference with correlation functions $\xi_\pm(\theta)$ and band powers $C_\ell$
  • Paper I: shape catalog (Hervas-Peters et al.)
  • Paper II: B-mode validation (Daley et al.)
  • Paper III: configuration-space constraints (Goh et al.)
  • Paper IV: harmonic-space constraints (Guerrini et al.)
  • The release — companion papers from one blinded catalog, built by about ten people over roughly a decade of survey development; one slide on each follows.
  • One redshift bin — only $r$-band across the footprint, so $n(z)$ comes from a SOM calibration on external deep fields; tomography comes at the end of the talk.
