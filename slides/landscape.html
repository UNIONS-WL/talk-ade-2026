UNIONS among weak-lensing surveys: Stage III area, in the north

  • Axes — survey area against effective source density; curves of constant total number of lensing sources (1 million to 1 billion).
  • Markers — the square's dot is the detection PSF FWHM; blue = measured, pink = forecast. Colour bars are each survey's wide-field bands.
  • UNIONS — area comparable to DES Y3, density a little higher, $ugriz$ from CFHT, Pan-STARRS and Subaru, all in the northern sky.
  • Stage IV — LSST, Euclid, Roman and CSST sit up and to the right; UNIONS is the large northern Stage III-scale sample before them.
